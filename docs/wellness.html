Focus and breaks

Under Wellness in the sidebar, Focus & breaks helps with long days at the screen: a focus timer, and reminders to rest your eyes, stretch, drink water and sit well. Everything is off until you start it or switch it on, and nothing leaves your machine.

Focus timer#

The Focus tab runs focus sessions and breaks in rounds, with a long break after the last round. A large ring fills as the time goes, in the tool's colour while you focus and a calm green during breaks; dots under the time show the round.

PresetFocusBreakLong breakRounds
Classic25 min5 min15 min4
Deep work50 min10 min20 min3
Flow90 min20 min30 min2
CustomYour own lengths and rounds, set with − and +
  • Start and pause with the round button or Space (when no button or field has focus); +5 min adds time, Skip moves to the next phase, Reset goes back to round 1.
  • What are you working on? Write it under the ring; it shows in the status bar popover and stays while the window is open.
  • When a phase ends a chime sounds and a card offers the next one (Start break, Skip break, Start focus). Start the next phase by itself skips the card.
  • The timer keeps running whichever tool is open, and after a reload. If the laptop slept through the end, it offers a fresh start instead of a late alarm.

The Today tab counts today's focus sessions, minutes focused and breaks, and draws them on a timeline of the day. It is kept only until midnight; Clear today empties it sooner.

Break reminders#

The Breaks tab holds reminders that run on their own, each switched on separately:

ReminderDefaultShows as
Eye breakevery 20 minutes, 20 seconds (the 20-20-20 rule)a card, with rings drifting into the distance
Stretchevery 50 minutes, 3 minutesthe whole window, with a figure doing five stretches
Waterevery hour, 20 secondsa card, with a glass that fills as it goes
Postureevery 30 minutes, 20 secondsa card, with a figure sitting up at a desk and three checks
Your owna name, a message, an interval and a lengthyour choice, with any of the pictures: breathing, look far, stretches, water, posture or none

Open a reminder's Settings to change its message, how often it comes and how long it lasts, how it shows (Full window, Card or Toast; a toast has no picture) and its sound. Each built-in reminder keeps its own picture; for your own you choose it (Breathing, Look far, Stretches, Water, Posture or none) and its sound; Try it shows it now. The breathing circle follows the rhythm under When to remind: calm 4-6, box 4-4-4-4 or 4-7-8, with the step and its seconds in words.

  • A break on screen can be snoozed for 5 minutes or skipped (Esc for a full-window one). Take a break now starts the longest one you have on.
  • Pause stops reminders for 30 minutes, an hour or until tomorrow; Resume starts their intervals over.
  • They work with the focus timer: none during a focus break (you are resting already), and with Wait until a focus session ends none during focus either.
  • Only during active hours keeps them to, say, 9:00–18:00 on weekdays.
  • One break shows at a time, and a break you take covers any shorter one that came due meanwhile. One that came due while the laptop slept is skipped, not shown late.

Sounds and notifications#

Under Sound choose the chime (soft bell, two notes, wood block, rising or gentle hum), its volume, or mute; each reminder can use its own or none. The chimes are made in the page, with no sound files. Last chime under Sound, and in the status-bar popover, says what rang last and when, so a sound is never a mystery.

With codec open in several tabs or windows, they share your choices as you change them, and one of them runs the reminders, so each comes once; when that one closes, another takes over. A focus session runs in the tab where you started it.

Notify me when codec is in the background shows a system notification when a phase ends or a reminder comes due and codec is not the window in front. The browser asks once; in the desktop app the notification comes from its built-in browser view.

From anywhere#

  • Status bar: while a session runs, a small ring and the time left; otherwise when the next reminder is due. Click it for start, pause, +5 min, skip, Break now and pausing reminders.
  • Window title: the time left while a session runs, so it shows on the taskbar or the browser tab.
  • Palette (Ctrl K): Focus: Start or pause, Skip, Add 5 minutes, Reset, Breaks: Take a break now, Pause reminders for an hour, Resume reminders.
  • Desktop tray menu: Start or pause focus, Take a break now, Pause reminders for an hour, Resume reminders.

What is kept#

Your choices (lengths, reminders, sounds), a running session (so a reload carries on) and today's totals are kept in the browser's storage, like the other display preferences; the task line only for the open window. There is no history beyond today, and nothing is sent anywhere. Reminders run while codec is open; for breaks away from codec altogether, a system-wide tool such as Stretchly or Workrave covers the other applications.
